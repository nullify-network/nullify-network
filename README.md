## ◆ What we do

We help teams find and close vulnerabilities **before someone exploits them**. No fear-mongering — just proactive protection of your infrastructure.

| | Service | What you get |
|:--:|---|---|
| 🛰️ | **AI Scanner** | Continuous automated scanning of domains & APIs, 24/7, AI-prioritized by real risk |
| 🔍 | **Manual Pentest** | Hands-on audit by certified engineers (OSCP/CEH): attack chains, logic flaws, business logic |
| 🧩 | **Enterprise** | Scanner + manual audit + dedicated security engineer, custom SLA |
| 🎯 | **Red Team / Bug Bounty** | Real-world attack simulation & private programs with vetted researchers |
